Measurement sources and limits

Measured angles come from the displayed geometry. Simulated values use the recorded motion and contacts. Force readings are averaged over each 1/60 second of simulation. Estimated masses and weights use the model’s material assumptions. Gold lines compare the chassis or board slope with horizontal. Rose arrows show force direction. Their length is for visibility. The recorded motions use rigid bodies and ideal joints.
